About Us

A neighbourhood that refused to look away, and built a promise instead.

Our Story

It Began With a Sight We Could Not Unsee

Akbar Suffa Welfare Society began in Baghban Pura, Lahore, with a painful reality: bodies lying unclaimed in hospital mortuaries, and funerals that never happened, because there was no family to arrange them, and no money to pay for them.

So the neighbourhood made a decision: not here. Not on our watch. Volunteers organised a burial service that treats every deceased person, known or unknown, rich or destitute, with the same honour Islam commands.

From that single act of dignity, the work kept growing: free education for poor children, monthly stipends for elderly mothers with no earner left, care and shelter for the abandoned old, a dispensary for the sick, and a dastarkhwan where anyone can sit down and eat with respect.

Our Mission

Three Values Run Everything We Do

Dignity

Every human being deserves honour, the orphan in the classroom, the widow at our door, and the stranger on his final journey.

Transparency

Your Zakat and Sadaqah are an amanah, a sacred trust. We distribute to verified deserving recipients and account for every rupee.

Compassion

We never ask who someone is or where they come from. Need is the only question we ask.
