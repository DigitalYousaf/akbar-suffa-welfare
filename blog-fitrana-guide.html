Fitrana: Rates, Rules and Who Receives It

What Fitrana is, when to pay it and the per-person rate, and how your Fitrah puts a full Eid meal on a poor family's table.

Ramadan Guide

Fitrana (Sadaqat-ul-Fitr) is the charity paid before the Eid-ul-Fitr prayer, so that the poor can eat, dress and celebrate on Eid day too. It is obligatory on every Muslim who has food beyond one day's need, paid for yourself and for your dependents.

How much? Fitrana equals the value of roughly 2.25 kg of wheat per person (or its equivalent in dates, barley or raisins). Scholars announce the official cash rate each Ramadan based on market prices; in recent years the wheat-based rate in Pakistan has been around Rs 300 per person. Please confirm the current year's rate when Ramadan begins.

When to pay? Any time during Ramadan, but the earlier the better, and always before the Eid prayer, so families can prepare. The Society distributes Fitrana as food packages and cash support to verified deserving families ahead of Eid day.

Who receives it? The same deserving categories as Zakat, the poor and the needy. Pay yours on our Fitrana page and we will deliver it before Eid, in sha Allah.
