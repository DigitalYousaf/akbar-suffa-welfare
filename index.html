Who We Are

Serving Humanity With Dignity & Responsibility

We are a community welfare society from Baghban Pura, Lahore. Our volunteers work day and night with local hospitals and police so that every person — rich or unknown — is treated with honour in life and in death.

  • Dignified burial for unclaimed & destitute deceased
  • Free education for underprivileged children
  • Monthly stipends for elderly & widowed women
  • Sasta Dastarkhwan — respectful, affordable daily meals

The Last Honour

He had no name in the hospital register. No family came. For three days, no one claimed him. Then our volunteers arrived — they bathed him with the same care as their own father, wrapped him in a clean kafan, carried him on their own shoulders, prayed his janaza with a full congregation, and laid him to rest with a marked grave.

This is what your Sadaqah does here. Every week, men and women who die unknown in Lahore are given the farewell every human being deserves.
