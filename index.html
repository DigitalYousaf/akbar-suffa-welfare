Who We Are

A Small Team With One Big Promise: Dignity for Every Human Being

From one neighbourhood in Lahore, our volunteers work with hospitals, police and local families so that nobody faces their hardest day alone — not in hunger, not in illness, and not in death.

  • Dignified burials for unclaimed and destitute deceased
  • Free education for children poverty would leave behind
  • Monthly stipends for elderly and widowed mothers
  • A dastarkhwan where everyone eats with respect

Our Signature Work

Aakhri Izzat — The Last Honour

He had no name in the hospital register. No family came forward. For three days, nobody claimed him. Then our volunteers arrived. They washed him with the care they would give their own father, wrapped him in a clean kafan, carried him on their own shoulders, gathered a congregation for his janaza — and buried him in a proper, marked grave.

Every week, men and women who die unknown in this city receive the farewell every human being deserves. This is what your Sadaqah does here.
