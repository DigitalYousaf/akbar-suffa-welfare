A True Story

A Grave With a Name

Last winter, an elderly man was found deceased near Shalimar. No ID. No phone. No family traced for days. Our team completed his ghusl and janaza, and then, with the help of a neighbour who finally recognised him, his grave was marked with his real name.

A name matters. It is the difference between being forgotten, and being remembered in people's prayers. Stories like his are why we exist, and why your support must never stop.

A registered welfare society serving Lahore with dignity, compassion and responsibility, dignified burials, free education, elderly care and daily meals for the deserving.
